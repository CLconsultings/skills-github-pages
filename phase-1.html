Phase 1 · AI Impact & Readiness

Turn an AI opportunity into a decision you can defend.

Phase 1 establishes whether the opportunity is worth pursuing, what has to be true for it to work, what evidence is missing, and what the organization should do next.

What Phase 1 evaluates

Business objective

Define the decision, intended outcome, scope, and economic or operational consequence.

Workflow fit

Examine the end-to-end workflow, constraints, handoffs, review burden, and where AI could actually change performance.

Evidence & measurement

Identify the starting baseline, available evidence, target outcome, and what would count as credible improvement.

Ownership & controls

Clarify human accountability, review authority, risk boundaries, escalation, and the controls required for responsible use.

What you receive

1. Decision framing

A clear statement of the business problem and the decision being made.

2. Constraint finding

The primary workflow, evidence, readiness, ownership, or control issue limiting progress.

3. Evidence requirements

The baseline, measures, thresholds, and verification needed to judge the result.

4. Recommended posture

A defensible next action: ACT, TEST, DEFER, or STOP, with the appropriate operating pathway.

The assessment is designed to support a decision, not guarantee implementation. If the evidence does not justify moving forward, stopping or gathering better evidence is a valid result.

Inputs → analysis → decision

You provide: the workflow or AI opportunity, available process information, relevant stakeholders, and any existing performance evidence.

CLConsulting evaluates: value potential, workflow condition, readiness, evidence quality, accountability, risk, and measurement feasibility.

You receive: a decision-ready assessment identifying the limiting constraint, evidence gaps, recommended posture, and smallest responsible next action.
