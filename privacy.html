Privacy

Privacy information

This notice describes what the current public website source code does with information entered into its browser-based tools and how direct contact works.

Current source-code behavior: the Executive Quick Signal and Decision Preview calculate results in your browser. The site code does not submit those answers to CLConsulting, store them in browser local/session storage, set application cookies, or send them to an analytics service.

Information you enter into browser-based tools

The Quick Signal includes optional participant and role/organization fields. In the current implementation, those fields and the assessment responses are used only in the page running in your browser. Refreshing or leaving the page clears the entered information unless your browser independently preserves form state.

Email contact

Links labeled for contact use your device's email application. If you choose to send an email, the information you include is transmitted through your email provider and the receiving email service rather than through the website's assessment code.

Hosting and network metadata

As with most websites, hosting, domain, network, or security providers may process standard request metadata needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address, request time, browser information, and requested page. Their handling is governed by their own service terms and privacy practices.

Client and project data

This public website notice does not authorize sending confidential, regulated, proprietary, or client-sensitive information through the browser-based demonstration tools. Project-specific data handling requirements should be established for the applicable engagement and systems before sensitive information is used with AI services.

Changes

This notice should be reviewed when the site adds analytics, server-side forms, new integrations, account features, persistent storage, or other functionality that changes how information is processed.
